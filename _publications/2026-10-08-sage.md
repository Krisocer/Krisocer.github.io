**Authors:** Zhimin Li, Pan Wang, Jingxian Chen, Yuantao Tang, Qian Lou, and Jingtong Hu

**Venue/status:** arXiv:2610.14089; under review at ICLR 2027

SAGE improves protected vision-language inference on smart glasses by reducing visual input cost, using shape-specialized INT8 execution, and transmitting compact protected representations. On real glasses, it reduces inference latency and protected packet size while retaining downstream answer quality.
