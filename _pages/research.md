## Privacy-preserving wearable vision-language models

**November 2025–present · Advisor: Jingtong Hu**

I study privacy risks in wearable AI, where intermediate visual representations can reveal sensitive attributes even when final answers appear harmless. I developed a split-inference approach that transforms visual tokens before they leave the trusted device. In a 3,221-record privacy–utility benchmark, private-attribute inference accuracy fell from 56.7% to 7.4% while relaxed utility remained at 74.4%.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28691)

## Fairness in AI-assisted mobile healthcare

**November 2023–November 2025 · Advisor: Jingtong Hu**

I worked on medical image synthesis and downstream diagnosis with demographic balance and clinician-informed preferences. FairGen improved fairness by 95.9% on skin images, 80.0% on chest radiography, and 35.2% on brain MRI while maintaining competitive diagnostic accuracy.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02868-z) · [Code](https://github.com/Krisocer/FairGen)

## Efficient real-time object detection on the edge

**March–November 2023 · Advisor: Yanzhi Wang**

I contributed to a deployment framework that coordinates video keyframe selection, CPU/GPU dynamic voltage and frequency scaling, and DNN pruning. It maintained object-detection accuracy within 0.28% mAP of processing every frame while reducing system power by up to 96.7%.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3676536.3676655)
